## About me

I am **Ravi Kumawat**, a BTech graduate from **IIT Gandhinagar** and a Full Stack Developer at **AIResQ Climsols Pvt. Ltd.**

I build technology for **urban resilience**, especially flood prediction, traffic analysis, geospatial applications, and smart mobility systems.

I worked with **MIR Lab** on a mobility platform that brings flood-risk prediction and traffic data together to support safer and more efficient routes during extreme conditions.
